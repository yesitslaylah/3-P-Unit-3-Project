Creamy Buldak Recipe 🔥

This is more a serving suggestion for buldak noodles! I used the extra hot chicken buldak noodles here (the ones in the black packet). And just to flag, these are sweat pulling hot… the cream and mozzarella manage to mute the heat slightly, but if you want to lean on the side of caution, only add a bit of the sauce packet (then add more if you can handle it)!

Recipe #1

  • Time: About 10 minutes
  • Serves: 1 (or 2 if you have a small appetite)
Ingredients Quantity
Garlic Just a few chops
Buldak Noodles (flavor up to you) One pack
Grated Mozzarella A handful
Double Cream A spoonful (to your liking)
Spring Onion A few chops

Optional Add-Ins

  • Boiled egg
  • Chili Flakes
  • Tofu

Step 1: Fry Garlic
In a medium sized frying pan over a medium heat, add 1 tbsp of oil then the garlic. Fry until golden, stir often.
