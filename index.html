Creamy Buldak Recipe 🔥

This is more a serving suggestion for buldak noodles! I used the extra hot chicken buldak noodles here (the ones in the black packet). And just to flag, these are sweat pulling hot… the cream and mozzarella manage to mute the heat slightly, but if you want to lean on the side of caution, only add a bit of the sauce packet (then add more if you can handle it)!
